Founded in Beverly Hills in 2011, Velocity Motors has grown from a boutique private collector garage into California's most trusted sanctuary for extraordinary motorcars.

Our Story

Redefining The Exotic Car Showroom Experience

Velocity Motors was established on a radical premise: automotive purchasing should never feel adversarial. For too long, the traditional dealership model traded in pressure and opacity. We envisioned a sanctuary where automotive enthusiasts, collectors, and drivers could explore extraordinary craftsmanship in an unhurried, gallery atmosphere.

Today, our climate-controlled private showroom houses over 50 hand-selected motorcars spanning vintage classics, high-octane track machines, executive flagships, and cutting-edge hyper-EVs. Each car is acquired through rigorous discernment and prepared without compromise.
